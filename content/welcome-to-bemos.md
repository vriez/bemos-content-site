# Welcome to BemOS Plantoes

This is the first post published through our brand-new content platform — and
the fact that you're reading it means the whole pipeline works end to end:
authored as markdown, committed to the repo, built by CI, and served live.

## Why this matters

Everything the business does rests on a **reliable publish-to-live path**. No
copy-pasting into a CMS, no manual FTP uploads, no "it works on my machine."
You write a post, push it, and it goes live automatically.

## How a post flows

Every post moves through a simple editorial workflow:

- **draft** — being written, not visible to the world
- **review** — ready for an editor's eyes
- **published** — live on the site

Only *published* posts render to the public site, so work-in-progress stays
private until it's ready.

## What's next

- Analytics and attribution so every post's performance is measurable
- Multi-channel distribution (email, social) from a single source of truth
- Scheduling so posts publish themselves at the right moment

This is the foundation. Everything else builds on top of it.
